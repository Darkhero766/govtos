# First Response — Product Context

## Surface
Public-facing citizen cyber-safety web experience. The primary surface is **brand / experience**, with focused product flows for reporting, evidence preparation, signal checking and complaint tracking.

## Audience
Indian internet users who may be stressed, confused or unfamiliar with cybercrime processes. The interface must work for first-time users on mobile, with plain English and Hindi support.

## Job to be done
Turn a confusing cyber incident into one clear next action: urgent financial response, safety planning, reporting, signal checking or complaint tracking.

## Product commitments
- Never imply that a synthetic/demo signal result proves safety.
- Put urgent financial-fraud action and 1930 guidance first when appropriate.
- Explain AI decisions in human-readable language.
- Preserve evidence-first behavior.
- Use official government channels for actual filing.
- Design for mobile before desktop.

## Emotional goal
The visitor should feel **"I know what to do next"**, not intimidated by a government form or overwhelmed by a cybersecurity dashboard.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, purple/blue AI gradients, glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, decorative grids, tiny low-contrast text, generic chatbot bubbles, and animation that feels like a floating toy.
