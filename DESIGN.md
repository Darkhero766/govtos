# First Response — Design Language

## Direction
A confident Indian public-service identity with an editorial, human tone. It should feel credible enough for a serious cyber-safety service while being warmer and more memorable than a conventional government portal.

## Palette
- Ink / navy: `#0F1428` — primary text, navigation, deep surfaces
- Red: `#D6432E` — urgent action and primary emphasis
- Amber: `#E8A33D` — secondary emphasis and physical offset accents
- Teal: `#2F9E8F` — safety, reassurance and positive states
- Warm paper: `#F4F1E9` — page background
- Soft cream: `#FBFAF5` — primary content surface

Do not introduce purple gradients, neon colors, or decorative glow systems.

## Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk, 700. Large, compressed-looking headlines with strong negative tracking.
- UI/body: IBM Plex Sans.
- Labels / references: IBM Plex Mono.
- Use real weight contrast: heavy display type against quiet body copy.
- Functional text should not fall below 11px.

## Composition
- Prefer asymmetry and strong editorial hierarchy over evenly distributed card grids.
- Hero art can sit slightly off-grid and occupy real visual space.
- Use hard or restrained radii (3–20px) rather than making every element pill-shaped.
- Use borders and structural spacing instead of persistent shadows.
- A decisive offset shadow may be used for key interactive moments.

## Components
- Primary CTA: solid navy/red/teal with short labels and clear verbs.
- Urgent action: red surface; 1930 should be visually unmistakable.
- Safety: teal surface.
- AI: useful tool, not a decorative chatbot bubble.
- Evidence: structured, quiet, scannable.
- Footer: dark ink with official-route disclosure.

## Motion
Motion communicates state or personality, never decoration. Officer art gets a subtle idle movement only. Avoid bounce, elastic easing, continuous hover, spinning, or attention-seeking animation.

## Mobile
Mobile is the primary constraint. Preserve headline hierarchy, keep primary actions thumb-friendly, prevent hero artwork from being cropped awkwardly, and avoid desktop layouts collapsing into cramped cards.

## Anti-patterns
Do not use:
- purple-to-blue AI gradients
- glassmorphism
- nested cards / card soup
- decorative CSS grid backgrounds
- excessive pills
- low-contrast gray-on-color text
- generic stock dashboard aesthetics
- floating character hover loops
